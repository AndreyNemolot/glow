# Clock

A night clock for the fold.

Set the phone on its cover and the hour is already there: large, pale, and still, on a black field. Open it, and the inner screen fills with slow light — a lamp the color of a room after dark.

Clock is made for foldable phones. The cover keeps the time. The inside keeps the light.

## The hour

The cover is one landscape face. The time is set in a thin, oversized type. Under it, the day, written out in the language of the phone. Beside it, the month: the weekdays, the grid, and today drawn as a fine circle.

The cover stays awake. Look across the room, and the hour is still there.

## The lamp

Open the phone and the inner display becomes light. Waves move across it, unhurried, the way light moves on water. It begins in warm amber. Press the color and drag — the lamp follows your finger through the whole wheel, and only the lamp changes.

Close the phone and the inner screen goes dark. The cover keeps the clock.

The lamp waits for a real opening, and it stays on until the phone is closed.

## Out of the way

Brightness and color are two quiet marks at the edge. Press one and a scale appears under your finger. Drag, then let go. A few seconds later the marks fade, and the face is only type again. Touch the glass and they return.

Through the night the whole face drifts a few pixels, so a bright shape never settles into the glass.

## Made for the fold

Clock is built around a cover display and a hinge. Open, and you have a lamp inside and the hour outside. Closed, and only the cover stays lit. The face is landscape, and it stays that way.

Leave it on the counter. The hour will still be there when you come back.
